Built for the way forwarders actually work

One record per load, with both sides of the deal: what the client pays you and what you pay the carrier.

Loads and margin

Route, dates, truck plate, client and carrier on one line. Your margin is worked out for every load and every month.

Payments and due dates

Due dates follow each client's and carrier's payment terms. Overdue invoices turn red, so you know whom to call today.

Transport orders

Print or save a transport order for the carrier straight from the load, with your company details already filled in.

Excel in, Excel out

Bring your clients, carriers and past loads from a spreadsheet. Export everything to Excel whenever you or your accountant need it.

Your whole office

Add colleagues as users. Everyone sees the same up-to-date list, and only the owner can change company settings.

CMR originals

See which signed CMRs are back from the carrier and which went on to the client. Photograph them with your phone and keep them with the load.
