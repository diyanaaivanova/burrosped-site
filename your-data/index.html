How we look after your data

What we do and do not do with the data of your company.

Support access is off

Our support team has no access to your data in the app. Only if you ask for help does the owner allow a read-only view for 24 hours or 7 days, and they can stop it at any time.

Every access on record

Every login, export and change, and every support visit, is written to an access log that owners see in Settings.

Hosted in the EU

Servers in the EU, encrypted nightly backups and a GDPR data processing agreement. Export everything or delete your account at any time.

How support access works

When you need help, you decide who looks, for how long, and you see every visit.

  1. You ask for help

    By email or phone. Until then support access is off.

  2. The owner allows it

    In Settings, for 24 hours or 7 days. Only an owner of your company can do this.

  3. We look, nothing more

    Support sees your data read-only. Nothing can be changed, exported or downloaded.

  4. It ends by itself

    After the chosen time, or as soon as you withdraw it. Every visit stays in your access log.

Every access on record

Owners see who logged in, what was exported or changed, and every visit from support, in Settings → Access log.

  • Logins, password changes and new users
  • Excel imports and downloads, changes to the settings
  • Every time support access is allowed, used or withdrawn
  • IP addresses shortened, entries kept for 12 months
